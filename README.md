# 🎤 LetSingOke

A modern karaoke player for Android. Sing along with your own song files, search karaoke videos on YouTube, get scored on your pitch, challenge a friend in Battle Mode, play music mini games, play classic arcade games, watch movies and live TV, and record your performance with video and lyrics.

**Current version:** 4.4.3 · versionCode 443

## ✨ Features

### Karaoke
- Karaoke player with synced lyrics, countdown, and adjustable lyric font and shadow
- Built-in song library, with song search, new songs, rankings, and a song book
- Add your own songs and files by importing them
- Singer 1 / Singer 2 modes for duets
- Tempo, key, and lyric-sync adjustments during playback
- Background visuals: videos, photos, or your camera
- Scoring with **Easy / Medium / Hard** difficulty, **Random** or **Microphone** score mode, and fanfare sounds
- Optional downloads: prebuilt songs, background photos, background videos, and soundfonts

### 🎬 YouTube Oke
Search karaoke videos on YouTube and queue them up.
- Queue, queue all, play next, skip, reorder, or clear the queue
- **Fair turns** mode rotates singers in the queue
- Favorites and saved playlists
- Backup code to copy and restore your data
- **Automatic picture quality** — the player reads your connection speed and picks the resolution
- Fast search: two requests instead of seven, with fallbacks only if both fail
- Cleaner playback: ad elements are hidden and skipped inside the player

### 🍿 Movie Oke
Browse, search, and watch movies and TV series.
- Trending, Movies, TV Series, and Downloads tabs
- **Continue Watching**: picks up where you stopped, with a Resume button and a progress bar
- Series remember your last episode and move on to the next one
- Subtitles, playback speed, quality selection, and episode picker
- **English subtitles and outline-only text on by default**, so a fresh install is readable on a TV
- **Scale option** in the player menu: Fit, Fill, or Stretch to your screen
- **Poster previews** while the library loads, with a proper D-pad dialog system
- **Adaptive quality cap** for low-RAM devices
- Download titles for offline viewing
- Fast, smooth browsing with instant loading from the last session and prefetching of details
- **Faster first open:** Movie Oke is warmed up in the background shortly after the app starts
- Full remote and D-pad support on TV

### 📺 IPTV Oke
Watch live channels with a country filter and full-screen playback.
- **Channel cards** with logo, name and group, instead of a plain list
- **Search and category filter** on the channel list
- **Philippine channels pinned to the top** (GMA, A2Z, Cinemo), with the Philippines list open by default
- **Direct playback** — no relay server or PC needed, with an automatic fallback if a link drops

### 🌐 Stream Oke
Watch streamed video inside the app, with a hub for every source: YouTube Oke, IPTV Oke, Movie Oke and Ani Oke.

### 🍥 Ani Oke
Anime and series playback with the same player controls as the rest of Stream Oke.
- Episode and season browsing, with the next episode one press away
- Subtitles and audio track selection per title
- Quality selection, with **DASH and HLS** playback for smooth streaming
- Full remote control: play / pause, seek, subtitles, audio, quality, next episode

### 🎮 Oke Arcade
Four music mini games, each playable on phone, tablet, and TV.
- **Guess Song:** name the tune
- **Beat Drop Oke:** a rhythm game for 1 or 2 players, Easy to Turbo difficulty, with Auto mode and a tutorial — now with a **built-in song panel** inside the mode
- **Pulse Oke:** a touch rhythm game with Easy to Fast difficulty, random song, and a tutorial
- **Pitch Perfect:** match the pitch, practice a specific part, or sing the full song — with the **song list beside the cards**, walkable with the D-pad
- Beat Drop Oke and Pulse Oke can be controlled from your phone using the Web Remote
- The hub now has a **Retro arcade** section with the **Arcade Oke** tile

### 🕹 Arcade Oke
Classic arcade and console games on the same TV, with the same remote.
- **5,000+ arcade titles** from the MAME 2003+ reference set — search a name and download it inside the app
- **Bring your own ROMs:** arcade sets (`.zip`), NES (`.nes`, `.fds`), GB / GBC / GBA, and SNES — or paste a download link
- **Three NES games included:** Concentration Room, LJ65, and AccuracyCoin
- Four emulator cores: **FBNeo, MAME 2003, MAME 2003+, and FCEUmm**
- **Clean screen** while playing, a **floating thumb stick** that appears wherever your thumb lands, and a six-button A–F pad
- **Pause menu** with Resume, Player settings, Save state, Load state, Restart game, and Exit — every item reachable with the remote's arrows and OK
- **Player settings:** volume and mute, shader, screen fit, FPS, fast-forward, VSync, save slot and auto-save, touch pad layout / side / size / opacity / vibration, phone controller, and key mapping
- The **TV remote is always Player 1**; Bluetooth gamepads are detected automatically
- **Two phones, two players** through the Web Remote arcade deck

### 🎯 Pitch Guide
A scrolling pitch bar shows the melody notes and your voice in real time.
- Octave-forgiving mode, so you can sing in your own octave
- Handles key shift (transpose)
- Mic is tuned to pick up your voice over the backing track (echo cancellation, noise suppression, adaptive noise gate)

### ⚔ Battle Mode
Two singers, one song, one turn each. The higher score wins.
- Enter both singers' names
- "You're up!" screen before each turn
- Result screen with winner, point margin, and Perfect / Good / Miss / Max combo
- Series tracker, rematch (swaps who sings first), or a new random song
- Open it from the Guess Song hub

### 🎙 Mic and Sound
- Live mic with voice effects (echo, reverb, clarity) and anti-feedback
- Pick your input: built-in, wired, USB / 2.4 GHz, or Bluetooth
- Sound Engine with soundfont support and an equalizer
- Mic check and live concert mode

### 📹 Recording
Record yourself singing with the camera, the backing track, and the lyrics overlay. If the live mic channel is silent, the app falls back to the mic so your voice always makes it into the recording.

### 🔗 Share Center
Share built-in songs, your own songs, your Song Book, and your video records. Shown on TV and set-top boxes.

### 📱 Web Remote
Control the app from a phone or any browser on the same network, and use your phone as a wireless mic.
- Browse songs, reserve them, and play now
- Control mic volume, tempo, and lyric timing
- Use the phone keyboard to type searches on a TV or box
- Generate a Song Book from your selected songs
- **Gamepad tab** — your phone becomes a controller for Oke Arcade and Arcade Oke
- **Arcade deck** with a landscape cabinet mode: drag the stick to move, A–F to play, COIN / START under the buttons, and Player 1 / Player 2 on the same deck
- **Add Files** on the phone sends songs, videos, photos and fonts straight to the TV
- No router? A phone hotspot works too

### 📖 How to use
A complete guide to every feature, built into the app — 14 sections, from adding your first song to troubleshooting. Always the last tile on the home screen.

### 📺 TV friendly
Works with remote and D-pad navigation on Android TV, TV boxes, and projectors. Devices without a touchscreen are treated as a TV automatically.

### 🔄 Updates
The app checks `manifest.json` in this repository for new versions (the `appUpdate` object: `versionCode`, `versionName`, `apkUrl`, `forceUpdate`, `changelog`). Use **Settings → Check for a newer version of LetSingOke**.

## 📲 Install

1. Download the latest APK [LetSingOke](https://github.com/morpius02-web/letsingoke/releases/download/v4.4.3/LetSingOke.v4.4.3.apk).
2. On your phone, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
3. Open the APK and install.
4. Open the app and go to settings and download the LetSingOke assets files.

**Requires:** Android 6.0 (API 23) or higher. About 2 GB of RAM is recommended; 3 GB or more is best for Movie Oke and the soundfont engine.

**Storage:** Arcade Oke adds around 20 MB of emulator cores and bundled games. Downloaded arcade titles and movies are stored separately, so keep some free space on the device.

## 🛠 Tips

- **Song leaks into the mic?** Lower the speaker volume or use a headset or wireless mic.
- **Wrong octave on the pitch bar?** Keep Octave-forgiving on in the Pitch Guide options.
- **No voice in a recording?** Check that the microphone permission is allowed, then try again.
- **Movie Oke search not working?** Check your internet connection and the device date and time, then try again.
- **Video does not fill the screen?** Open the player menu and change **Scale** to Fill or Stretch.
- **An arcade game will not start?** Arcade sets must be the matching MAME 2003+ zip — re-download it with **Get games**.
- **Arcade game runs slow?** Close other apps, and keep save states on internal storage rather than a slow USB stick.

## 🧱 Build

The project is a decoded APK (apktool). To rebuild:

```bash
apktool b <project-folder> -o LetSingOke-4.4.3.apk -f
# then zipalign and sign with your own keystore
```

> Use `-f` whenever only `apktool.yml` changed — without it apktool reuses the cached manifest and the version number stays at the old value.

The version lives in `apktool.yml`:

```yaml
versionInfo:
  versionCode: 443
  versionName: 4.4.3
```
